1. Introduction

Hilaac Academy is an online learning platform that provides courses, lessons, assessments, certificates, and educational services to students worldwide. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your data when you use Hilaac Academy.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Full name
  • Username
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Profile information (photo, bio, and other details you provide)

Learning Information

  • Courses enrolled
  • Assignments submitted
  • Quiz results and assessment scores
  • Certificates earned
  • Course progress and lesson completion data

Technical Information

  • Browser and device information
  • Login activity and session data
  • IP address and general system usage data
  • Security and audit logs for platform protection

3. How We Use Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide learning services and deliver course content
  • Manage student and instructor accounts
  • Track course progress and learning outcomes
  • Improve platform performance and user experience
  • Send important notifications about your account, courses, and platform updates
  • Monitor security, prevent fraud, and maintain a safe learning environment
  • Process enrollments and payments related to courses

4. Account Security

Your account is protected by secure authentication and password encryption. You are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential and for all activity that occurs under your account.

If you suspect unauthorized access, contact Hilaac Academy support immediately so we can help secure your account.

5. Payment Information

Payment details are processed through secure payment channels. Hilaac Academy does not store full payment card details on our servers and does not misuse payment information.

Payment records may be retained for accounting, enrollment verification, and support purposes in accordance with applicable regulations.

6. Data Storage

User data is stored securely on protected servers. We take reasonable measures to prevent unauthorized access, loss, or misuse of your information.

Data is only removed when:

  • You request account deletion or data assistance through support
  • An authorized administrator deletes an account according to platform rules and policies
  • Retention is no longer required for legal or operational purposes

7. Cookies and Tracking

Hilaac Academy may use cookies and similar technologies to maintain your session, remember preferences, and understand how the platform is used. These tools help us improve navigation, security, and overall service quality.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

8. Your Rights

As a Hilaac Academy user, you can:

  • Update your profile information from your account settings
  • Change your password at any time
  • Request account assistance from our support team
  • Control the personal information you choose to share on your profile
  • Ask questions about how your data is used
